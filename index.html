The Playing Area

Label three spots in the room before you start.

🍎 Fruit One side of the room
💩 Poop The center
🍨 Scoop The other side

How to Play

  1. Mark the room: Fruit on one side, Scoop on the other, Poop in the center.
  2. Everyone starts scattered around the playing area.
  3. The caller shouts a location. Players sprint to that spot.
  4. The last one to make it is out.
  5. Drop in the bonus commands below to keep everyone guessing.

Commands

Fruit

Everyone races to the Fruit side.

Scoop

Everyone races to the Scoop side.

Poop

Everyone races to the center.

Hit the Deck

Everyone drops to the floor. The last one down is out.

Captain’s Coming

Everyone stands still and salutes — and keeps saluting until “At Ease” is called.

At Ease

Stop saluting. No other command works until “At Ease” has been called.

3 Men Rowing

Get into groups of three, sitting one behind the other, and pretend to row. Anyone left without a trio is out. (The number can be changed at the whim of the host.)

Tips & Tricks

  • Call it loud and fast. Hesitation kills the energy.
  • Don’t repeat yourself. Keep the next command a surprise.
  • Save “At Ease.” It’s your unlock button — nobody can obey anything else until it lands.
  • Mix in “Captain’s Coming” mid-chaos. Freezing a room that was just sprinting is the whole joke.
  • Adjust the rowing number. Three is a suggestion, not a law.
  • Keep it short. This game burns hot — a few rounds is plenty.
